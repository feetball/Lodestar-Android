Lodestar turns the phone on your camera into a smart finder for night photography. Pick a planet, comet, nebula, galaxy or star, and on-screen arrows tell you which way to pan and tilt the tripod head until it sits in your frame.

GUIDED AIMING
• A live finder with a virtual sky or the camera view, the target marker and an arrow pointing the shortest way
• "Pan right 12.6° · Tilt up 12.9°" read-outs for pan-tilt heads, the distance to go and a clear on-target signal with haptics
• The outline of your own camera's frame, from its sensor size and focal length
• Optional audio ticks that speed up as you get closer, and a level gauge

ACCURACY BEYOND THE COMPASS
• Star alignment: centre a bright star in your camera once and Lodestar corrects the compass and the bracket
• Gyro Lock keeps the heading on the gyroscope, safe from the magnetism of camera bodies and tripods
• Works with the phone mounted in portrait, in landscape, upside down or flat on top of the camera

FIND YOUR TARGET
• Search and browse 9,000+ stars, 690+ galaxies, nebulae and clusters including all 110 Messier objects, the planets, the Moon, the Milky Way core, and comets and bright asteroids
• Sort by name, brightness, altitude or rise time; favourites, recents and custom coordinates
• Each object shows where it is now, when it rises and sets, an altitude chart for the night, how it fits your frame and the longest exposure before stars trail; comets also show which way the tail points

PLAN THE NIGHT
• Tonight: sunset, twilight and darkness windows, the Moon's phase and hours, when the Milky Way core is up, the visible planets and the best targets
• An events calendar: meteor showers, lunar and solar eclipses, conjunctions, oppositions, supermoons and more, with when and where to look from your site
• A mosaic planner for panoramas such as the Milky Way arch, and star-trail and time-lapse planners
• Record your site's real skyline, so rise and set times respect trees and mountains
• An all-sky chart with constellations and the Milky Way, with time travel, and a polar-scope clock for star trackers

GOTO MOUNTS
• Send a GoTo mount to your target over Wi-Fi: OnStep and LX200, Celestron NexStar and SynScan, ASCOM Alpaca, and the Sky-Watcher AZ-GTi and Star Adventurer GTi
• Sync on a centred star, nudge with arrows, and a Stop button that is always there

AT A GLANCE
• Home-screen widgets with tonight's dark hours, the Moon, the Milky Way core and the next event

OPTIONAL ONLINE DATA
Off until you turn it on: ISS and bright satellite passes you can follow in the finder, Starlink streak warnings for your exposure, fresh comet orbits and tonight's cloud forecast.

MADE FOR THE DARK
• A pure red night-vision mode, automatic after sunset, with screen dimming
• Everything works offline at remote dark-sky sites

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. Your location and camera picture stay on your phone.
