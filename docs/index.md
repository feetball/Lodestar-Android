# Lodestar for Android

**Aim your camera at the stars.** Lodestar turns the phone on your camera into a smart finder for night
photography: pick a planet, nebula, galaxy or star, and on-screen arrows show which way to pan and tilt your
tripod head until it sits in your frame.

- Guided aiming with a virtual sky or the camera view, star alignment and Gyro Lock
- 9,000+ stars, 690+ deep-sky objects including all 110 Messier objects, the planets, the Moon and the Milky Way core
- Tonight's darkness windows, Moon, Milky Way core and best targets; an all-sky chart with time travel
- A pure red night-vision mode; everything works offline
