# Support

Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome on the project's issue tracker:
<https://github.com/feetball/Lodestar-Android/issues>, or by email to
[feetball@gmail.com](mailto:feetball@gmail.com).

## Tips

- **Mount the phone rigidly** on the camera with its camera parallel to the lens, then choose the matching mount
  in Settings › Mount.
- **Align on a star** (Aim › Align) for accuracy beyond the compass, and use **Gyro Lock** near metal tripods.
- **Calibrate the compass** by moving the phone in a figure eight if Lodestar reports low compass accuracy.
- **Night mode** turns the whole app red. For extra protection, also turn on Android's *Extra dim* (Settings ›
  Accessibility) and lower the brightness.
